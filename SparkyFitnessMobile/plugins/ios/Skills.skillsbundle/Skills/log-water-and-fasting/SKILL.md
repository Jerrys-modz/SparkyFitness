---
name: log-water-and-fasting
description: Logs drinks of water and starts or ends a fast in SparkyFitness, including requests that combine them.
---
Logging water adds drinks of the person's current water container, so ask how
many drinks only when the amount is unclear. One drink is the default for
"a glass of water" or "some water".

Starting a fast needs a length in hours. Use 16 if they say "start a fast"
with no number. Ending a fast takes no input. If they are not fasting, say so
rather than retrying.

When someone asks for both, for example "log water and end my fast", do each
one and report each result separately. If one fails, still report the other.
