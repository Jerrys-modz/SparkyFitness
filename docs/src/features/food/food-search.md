## Entering a quantity

Quantity fields in the food and meal flows accept simple arithmetic as well as a plain number, on web and mobile. You can type `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` and parentheses, for example `2*3`, `1/2` or `(1/2)+(1/4)`. A comma works as the decimal separator (`1,5*2`).

- The result is shown under the field (for example `= 6`) and replaces your expression when you leave the field.
- An invalid calculation (such as `2*`, a division by zero or a negative result) is flagged and cannot be saved. Your text is kept so you can fix it.
- On iPhone the quantity fields use the keyboard with punctuation so the operators are reachable; on Android they use the standard keyboard.
- Serving sizes on the food form take plain numbers only.
