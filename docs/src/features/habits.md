# Habits & Supplements

The **Habits** page is a daily checklist for things you want to do every day, such as taking a supplement, stretching or meditating.

- **Add a habit** with the field at the bottom of the checklist (up to 50 characters).
- **Tick it off** for the selected day. Use the day navigator to fill in earlier days.
- **Streaks** show how many days in a row you have completed a habit. A day you have not ticked yet does not break the streak until it has passed.
- **Delete** a habit to remove it together with its history.

Habits are stored as yes/no custom check-in categories, so they follow the same sharing rules as other check-in data (see [Family & Friends Sharing](/features/family-friends-sharing)) and are also available to the AI assistant and the MCP server.

For supplements with dosing, schedules and nutrient contributions, use [Medications](/features/check-in) instead.
