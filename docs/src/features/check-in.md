# Check-In

The Check-In feature in SparkyFitness allows you to quickly log your daily progress, including meals, exercises, and other key metrics. Detailed instructions on using the Check-In feature will be provided in the future.

## Mood

Log how you feel from the Check-In page by picking a mood face. You can also rate your Energy (Drained to Energized) and your Focus (Scattered to Locked in) from 1 to 5. Both ratings are optional, and tapping the selected rating again clears it.
Add tags and write a Journal entry (optional) to give the check-in some context.

### Several check-ins a day

On the web Check-In page, open the **Fasting & Mood** tab. The left card is the composer, titled **New check-in** (or **Edit check-in** while you change one). It has a Time field, the mood picker, Energy, Focus, tags and a Journal. The Time field stays on the current time until you set it yourself. Every field is optional, but a check-in needs at least one of them.

The right side lists the day's check-ins in time order, with the day's average mood, energy and focus above the list. Each check-in has an edit button and a delete button, and deleting asks you to confirm. A check-in without a mood is listed without a face and is left out of the average mood. The fasting card sits under the list.

Moods imported from Garmin or a file are not listed here and can't be edited. In Reports, the mood chart shows one value per day: the average of the moods you logged that day, or the Garmin or imported mood when you logged none. Its tooltip shows how many check-ins the day has.
