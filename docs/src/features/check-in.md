# Check-In

The Check-In feature in SparkyFitness allows you to quickly log your daily progress, including meals, exercises, and other key metrics. Detailed instructions on using the Check-In feature will be provided in the future.

## Mood

Log how you feel from the Check-In page by picking a mood face. You can also rate your Energy (Drained to Energized) and your Focus (Scattered to Locked in) from 1 to 5. Both ratings are optional, and tapping the selected rating again clears it.
Add tags and write a Journal entry (optional) to give the check-in some context.

The API supports several mood check-ins a day, each with its own time and every field optional; the web app and the mobile app will follow. Until then they keep saving one mood per day.
