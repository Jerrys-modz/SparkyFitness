# Fueling Plan

Work out how many carbohydrates to take per hour on a long run or race, how big each portion is, and how often to take one.

::: warning General guidance, not medical advice
These numbers are starting points taken from published sports-nutrition guidance. Everyone's gut, effort and conditions differ. Practise your plan in training before race day, and talk to a clinician or sports dietitian if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medication.
:::

---

## Where to find it

On the web, open the **Exercises** page and scroll to **Fueling plan**. Enter your duration, effort and gut training to see the plan.

## How the target is chosen

The default target depends on how long you will be running:

| Duration                         | Default target                      |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Under 60 minutes                 | No fueling suggested                |
| 60 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes | 30 to 60 g of carbohydrate per hour |
| Over 2 hours 30 minutes          | 60 to 90 g per hour                 |

Within the range, easy efforts (heart rate zone 1) aim at the low end and hard efforts (zone 5) at the high end. The default is also capped by how well your gut is trained:

| Gut training | Highest default |
| ------------ | --------------- |
| Beginner     | 45 g per hour   |
| Intermediate | 60 g per hour   |
| Advanced     | 90 g per hour   |

Higher intakes work best with a mix of glucose and fructose, and need practice. Start low and build up across your training block.

## Portion size and reminders

- The default portion is 25 g of carbohydrate, about one typical energy gel. Change it to match the product you carry.
- The time between portions is the portion size divided by your hourly target. For example, 25 g portions at 50 g per hour means one every 30 minutes.
- The first reminder comes within the first 45 minutes, then repeats at that interval until the planned finish.

## Your own numbers

You can replace the hourly target and the portion size with your own values. An override is used as typed, up to 120 g per hour and 100 g per portion. Your plan then shows the default range for the duration next to your number so you can see how far from the usual guidance it is.

## Sources
