# Bike Inventory

Keep a list of your bikes, see how far each one has gone, and track the wear on its parts (chain, tires, brake pads and so on) so you know when to replace them.

Bikes sit next to the [shoe inventory](/features/exercises/shoe-inventory) in the same **Equipment** section. Your shoes are not affected.

---

## Adding a bike

1. Open **Equipment**: on the web it is at the bottom of the **Exercises** page; in the mobile app it is **Library > Equipment > Bikes**.
2. Tap **Add bike**. **Name** is the only required field. Brand and model are optional.
3. **Starting distance** is for a bike that already has some distance on it when you add it.
4. Switch on **Use as my default bike** to have rides recorded in the mobile app (outdoor GPS rides) logged with that bike. Only one bike can be the default.

Distances follow your distance unit and are stored in kilometres.

## Linking a ride to a bike

- **Mobile:** open the ride from your diary and use **Bike**. Rides recorded in the app are saved with your default bike automatically. Rides show **Bike** instead of **Shoe**; every other cardio activity keeps the shoe picker.
- **Web:** open a ride from your diary, pick the bike under **Bike**, and save.

A bike's distance and time are its starting distance plus every ride linked to it (moving time, or the logged duration when there is none). Changing or removing the link, editing the distance, or deleting the ride updates the totals.

## Components and wear

Each bike has its own components. Open a bike in the mobile app and tap **Add component**:

- **Type**: chain, cassette, tires, brake pads, cables, bar tape or custom.
- **Installed on**: rides on this bike from that day on count towards the part.
- **Expected life**: in distance, hours, or both. A new part starts from a default for its type (for example 4,000 km for a chain), which you can change. With both set the part is due when either is reached.

The wear bar shows how much of the expected life is used. A part is **Due soon** from 80% and **Due now** from 100%; the bar stops at 100% but the percentage keeps counting. The bike list shows how many parts are due.

### Replacing and retiring

**Replace** retires the old part and fits a new one of the same type, starting from zero today. The old part moves to the bike's **History** with the distance it covered, so nothing is lost. A ride on the day of the swap counts for the new part. **Retire** moves a part to the history without fitting a new one. Use **Delete** on a part only to remove a mistake: its wear is lost.

## Retiring or deleting a bike

**Retire** moves a bike to the retired list, clears its default flag and stops it appearing in the bike picker unless a ride is already linked to it. You can **Reactivate** it later. **Delete** removes the bike and its components; rides stay in your diary but are no longer linked to a bike.

## Web and mobile

The web app lists your bikes with their distance, time and how many parts are due, and lets you add, edit, retire and delete bikes and pick a bike on a ride. Adding, replacing and retiring components is done in the mobile app.

## Family access

Bikes follow the exercise diary. A family member with **Manage Diary** access can add bikes and components and link rides on your behalf; **View Reports** access can see them.
