# Warm-Up Sets

Add a ramp of warm-up sets to a lift in one tap, instead of typing each one in between sets.

## Add warm-ups

In an active workout on the mobile app, open the **…** menu on an exercise and choose **Add warm-ups**. The sets are built from the weight of the exercise's first working set, whether you typed it or it is the suggested weight shown in grey.

| Set | Weight | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The empty bar: 20 kg or 45 lb | 10 |
| 2 | 50% of the working weight | 5 |
| 3 | 70% | 3 |
| 4 | 85% | 1 |

Weights are rounded to a loadable step in your unit (2.5 kg or 5 lb). A step that would not climb above the one before it, or that reaches the working weight, is left out, so a lighter lift gets a shorter ramp. If the working weight is not above the bar there is nothing to add.

## How the sets behave

- They use the **Warm-up** set type, so they stay out of volume, personal records and progression, the same as warm-ups you add by hand. They stay editable, and you log them like any other set.
- Each has a 60 second rest.
- Tapping **Add warm-ups** again replaces warm-ups you have not logged yet instead of adding more. Once you have logged a warm-up, the option is hidden for that exercise.
- The option is not shown for cardio or timed exercises, or when the first working set has no weight yet.
