# Route History and Racing Your Best Time

A saved route remembers the runs and walks you did on it. Open the route to see how many times you've done it, your best and last time, whether your latest run was faster or slower than your recent ones, and every run listed with its time, distance, pace and average heart rate. Tap a run to open it.

Nothing here needs a setting, a watch or an outside service. It is built from the activities already in your diary.

---

## Recording a run on a route

1. Open a saved route (**Library → Routes**) and tap **Record a run on this route**. This is offered for run, walk and hike routes, not rides.
2. The Record screen opens with the route named at the top. Choose walk or run, outdoor, and tap **Start**.
3. Record as usual. When you save the activity, SparkyFitness links it to the route.

The run is an ordinary diary activity. The link is only the route it followed; it does not check that you actually stayed on the line. Indoor sessions are never linked.

The routes list shows **Ran N times** under each route you've run, and the run summary shows **Followed _route name_** with a note when you beat your previous best on that route.

If you delete a route, the activities stay in your diary; they just stop being linked to anything.

---

## Racing your best time

When you record a run on a route that already has a timed run, the live screen shows how you are doing against your best time on it, right under the clock:

- **Ahead 12 s** (green) or **Behind 8 s** (orange), updated as you go.
- The first run on a route has nothing to race, so nothing is shown.

The comparison uses the distance your phone has recorded, matched to where your best run was at the same distance. If that run has a GPS track, its pace through the run (fast early, slower late) is used. If it has none, an even pace over its total time is assumed. Because it goes by recorded distance, GPS drift over a long run can shift the figure by a few seconds. It disappears once you pass the route's length.

The best time is the shortest logged duration over the route. Pauses are not counted in an activity's duration.

---

## Privacy

Routes and their history are personal. Only you can open a route or link an activity to it: they are never shared with family or friends, even with delegated access, and nothing is sent to any outside service. See [Family & Friends Sharing](/features/family-friends-sharing).

---

## Not included

Following a route with turn-by-turn guidance, racing on an Apple Watch, and a map of everywhere you've run on a route are not part of this feature.
