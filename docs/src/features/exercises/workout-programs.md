# Workout Programs

New to strength training? Pick a ready-made program, add it in one step, and SparkyFitness sets up the workout presets and a plan that rotates through them. Each exercise has a rep range, and the app suggests a little more weight once you reach the top of it on every set.

The same programs are on web and mobile, and the Sparky assistant can recommend and add one for you.

---

## Finding it

- **Web:** open the **Exercises** page and use **Browse programs** in the **Ready-made workout programs** section, or go to `/exercises/workout-splits`.
- **Mobile:** **Library > Workout programs**.

Adding a program changes your own library and plans, so it is not available while you are acting on behalf of a family member.

## Choosing a program

Narrow the list with the filters at the top:

- **Where I train** is what equipment you have. A bigger setup can also do the smaller ones, so **Garage gym** also shows the home programs, and **Gym** shows everything.
  - **At home:** dumbbells, a bench and a pull-up bar.
  - **Garage gym:** adds a barbell and a squat rack.
  - **Gym:** adds machines and cables.
- **Level:** beginner or intermediate.
- **Goal:** build muscle or gain strength.
- **Days a week:** how often you want to train.

Select **Show sessions** on a program to see every session, with each exercise's sets and reps. Exercises show as plain names from the free exercise database until you add the program, then they appear in your library with their images.

## Adding a program

1. Choose **Add to my workouts** on a program.
2. Give the plan a name. It starts as the program's name.
3. Choose whether to **Make it my active plan**. Leave it off to keep your current plan.
4. Read and accept the medical disclaimer: check with a doctor or healthcare professional before starting a new workout program, especially with a medical condition or an injury. A program does not replace medical advice.
5. Choose **Add program**.

This adds the program's exercises to your exercise library (the ones you do not already have), one workout preset for each session, and a **sequential** workout plan that runs the sessions in order and then starts over, however many days a week you train. Starting weights are left empty: you enter them as you log your first workout, and the progression suggestions follow from there.

You can change anything afterwards. The presets and plan are ordinary ones, so edit, rename or delete them like your own.

::: info
The exercises come from the free exercise database. If it cannot be reached, nothing is added and you see an error. Try again once you are connected; exercises already added are not added twice.
:::

## How progression works

- Most exercises have a rep range, such as 3 sets of 8 to 12. When you reach the top of the range on every set, the next session suggests a little more weight (2.5 kg for upper-body lifts, 5 kg for the big lower-body lifts).
- **Linear 5x5** keeps the reps fixed at five and adds weight every session.
- Planks and other holds are timed in seconds and you progress them yourself.

Weights are stored in kilograms and shown in your preferred unit.

## With the Sparky assistant

Ask the assistant to recommend a program ("I have dumbbells and a bench at home and can train three days a week") and it lists the programs that fit, shows the sessions of one, and adds it for you once you agree. It reminds you about the medical disclaimer before it adds anything. Programs can be listed and viewed through the [MCP server](/features/mcp-server) with a read-only key, but adding one needs a key that can write.
